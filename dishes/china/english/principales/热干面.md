# 热干面的做法

预估烹饪难度：★★★

## Required Ingredients and Equipment

* 热干面特有的Alkaline Noodles
* Scallion
* Sour Beans
* Minced Meat
* 蒜水
* 肉汤汁
* Dried Radish
* Sesame Seeds酱
* Chili Oil
* Black Pepper Powder
* Soy Sauce
* 食Salt
* Chicken Bouillon

## Calculations

每份：

* 热干面特有的Alkaline Noodles (250g)
* Scallion (10g)
* Sour Beans (20g)
* Minced Meat (30g)
* 蒜水 (30ml)
* 肉汤汁 (30ml)
* Dried Radish (50g)
* Sesame Seeds酱 (40ml)
* Chili Oil (0-10ml)
* Black Pepper Powder(0-10g)
* Soy Sauce(5ml)
* 食Salt(3g)
* Chicken Bouillon(0-3g)

## Instructions

* 水煮沸，并加入Alkaline Noodles，焯烫 25 秒钟捞起
* 撒上食Salt、Chicken Bouillon和Black Pepper Powder
* Sesame Seeds酱用 90ml 水稀释，搅匀，然后加入
* 加入 5ml Soy Sauce，加入 30ml 肉汤汁和蒜水
* 加入Dried Radish，Minced Meat，Sour Beans，Chopped Scallion
* 拌均匀后开吃

## Additional Notes

* Chili Oil看个人口味添加
