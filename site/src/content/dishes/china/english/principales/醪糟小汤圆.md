# Fermented Rice Wine Glutinous Rice (Laozao)小汤圆的做法

预估烹饪难度：★★

## Required Ingredients and Equipment

* 小汤圆
* Fermented Rice Wine Glutinous Rice (Laozao)
* White Sugar
* Goji Berries（可选）

## Calculations

每次制作前需要确定计划做几份。1 serving正好够 1-2 个人食用

总量：

* 水 300ml * number of servings
* 小汤圆 250g * number of servings
* Fermented Rice Wine Glutinous Rice (Laozao) 50g * number of servings
* Goji Berries 5 颗 * number of servings
* White Sugar

## Instructions

* 将水倒入锅中并煮沸
* 放入小汤圆煮 8min
* 放入Fermented Rice Wine Glutinous Rice (Laozao)和Goji Berries再煮 2min
* 盛入Bowl中根据个人口味加入White Sugar并搅拌均匀
* 吃

## Additional Notes

* White Sugar的用量根据个人口味更改
* 若喜欢更浓的口感可以减少 100 毫升内的水
* 若喜欢软点的Goji Berries口感，可以将Goji Berries和小汤圆同时煮
